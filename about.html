ABOUT THE PORTAL

THE IDEA

Most of the web has quietly stopped supporting anything older than a few years. If your browser is old, whole sites refuse you entry - or worse, they load as a blank wall. This site is a small protest against that. It is built to the lowest common denominator worth supporting: Internet Explorer 5.5 on Windows 98. That means it also loads cleanly in RetroZilla, Netscape 4.7, and, of course, today's browsers.

The rules are simple:

» Layout with tables, not flexbox or grid.
» CSS 1 only; every style has an HTML-attribute fallback.
» Web-safe colors and system fonts only.
» GIF images instead of PNG-24 alpha.
» Zero JavaScript. If it needs a script, it does not ship.
» Fixed 760px layout that fits an 800x600 screen.
