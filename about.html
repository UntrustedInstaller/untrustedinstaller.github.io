About This Site

The Idea

Most of the modern web is a walled garden: if your browser is older than a few years, you are locked out. This site is a small protest against that. It is built to the lowest common denominator worth supporting - Internet Explorer 5.5 on Windows 98 - which means it also loads cleanly in RetroZilla, Netscape 4.7, and, of course, today's browsers.

The rules are simple:

» Layout with tables, not flexbox or grid.
» CSS 1 only; every style has an HTML-attribute fallback.
» Web-safe colors and system fonts only.
» GIF (1-bit transparency) instead of PNG-24 alpha.
» Zero JavaScript. If it needs a script, it does not ship.
» Fixed 760px layout that fits an 800x600 screen.

About the Operator

Greetings. I am untrustedinstaller, a collector and tinkerer with a soft spot for the machines and software of the late 1990s. When I am not dragging Windows 98 boxes out of storage, I spend time playing metal and swooning over cars.

My motto: just because the box is slow does not mean it has to be ugly.
